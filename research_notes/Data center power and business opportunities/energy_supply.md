# Data Center Energy Supply (as of Oct 2026): Solar, Wind, BESS, PPAs, 24/7 CFE, Gas, Nuclear, Behind-the-Meter

Research scope: global, with emphasis on India and the GCC. 17 web searches were run, no full-page fetches. Most facts come from search-result summaries of trade press (pv magazine, DCD, Utility Dive, World Nuclear News, POWER, ESG Today). Read the "Gaps" sections before citing anything as definitive.

## 1. Corporate PPAs: hyperscaler procurement volumes, Google 24/7 CFE, and RTC/FDRE PPAs in India

### Takeaway
Corporate clean-PPA volume fell for the first time in about a decade in 2025 (about 55.9 GW, down from a record 62 GW in 2024). The four hyperscalers made up about half of it, with Meta (10.24 GW) and Amazon (10.22 GW) leading. India's data-center PPAs are still mostly mid-sized solar or solar-wind captive/group-captive deals (20–840 MW). True RTC/FDRE contracts with data-center developers are being discussed but are poorly documented in public sources.

### Cited Findings
**Global volumes (BloombergNEF, 1H 2026 Corporate Energy Market Outlook)**
- Corporate buyers signed about 55.9 GW of clean PPAs in 2025, down from a record 62 GW in 2024. This was the first annual decline in roughly a decade. — [ESG News](https://esgnews.com/bloombergnef-report-shows-corporate-clean-energy-buying-declined-in-2025/); [Utility Dive](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/corporate-clean-energy-procurement-ppa-meta-amazon-google/812591/)
- Amazon, Meta, Google and Microsoft together took 49% of global corporate PPA volume in 2025. — [ESG News](https://esgnews.com/bloombergnef-report-shows-corporate-clean-energy-buying-declined-in-2025/); [ESG Today](https://www.esgtoday.com/amazon-meta-google-microsoft-account-for-half-of-global-clean-energy-purchase-deals-in-2025-report/)
- Meta contracted 10.24 GW and Amazon 10.22 GW in 2025. Together they took 20.4 GW, including 4.7 GW of nuclear. — [ESG Today](https://www.esgtoday.com/amazon-meta-google-microsoft-account-for-half-of-global-clean-energy-purchase-deals-in-2025-report/)
- U.S. corporate PPA volume hit a record 29.5 GW in 2025, but the number of unique U.S. buyers roughly halved to 33. This points to concentration among Big Tech buyers. — [ESG Today](https://www.esgtoday.com/amazon-meta-google-microsoft-account-for-half-of-global-clean-energy-purchase-deals-in-2025-report/)
- EMEA corporate PPA volume fell 13% to about 17 GW. — [Energy Institute / New Energy World](https://knowledge.energyinst.org/new-energy-world/article?id=140161)
- pv magazine's headline: corporate PPA deals were down 10% in 2025, with "AI demand plugging gaps". — [pv magazine](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/02/20/corporate-ppa-deals-down-10-in-2025-as-ai-demand-plugs-gaps/)
- A secondary aggregator lists cumulative portfolios (Amazon about 42 GW, Microsoft about 40 GW, Google about 35 GW, about 130 GW combined for the four). These are not BNEF-verified and should be treated as indicative. — [Axis Intelligence](https://axis-intelligence.com/big-tech-clean-energy/)

**Google 24/7 CFE**
- Google's goal is 24/7 carbon-free energy (hourly matching on every grid where it operates) by 2030. — [Data Center Frontier](https://www.datacenterfrontier.com/energy/article/11428734/google-our-data-centers-will-be-carbon-free-round-the-clock-by-2030)
- In 2023, Google reached 64% CFE globally on an hourly basis. Ten regions were at 90% or more and thirteen at 85% or more. — [ESIG presentation (Google)](https://www.esig.energy/attachment/serve/session-3b-the-path-to-24-7-carbon-free-energy-allegra-reister-2)
- Regional spread is very wide: Finland is around 97%, while Japan, Taiwan and Singapore are below 20%. — [edie](https://www.edie.net/google-posts-strong-progress-with-24-7-carbon-free-energy-goal/)
- Google publishes per-region hourly CFE% for its cloud regions, defined as the average hourly share of carbon-free energy including Google's own procurement in that grid. — [Google Cloud region carbon page](https://cloud.google.com/sustainability/region-carbon?hl=de)

**India data-center PPAs**
- Digital Edge signed a PPA with Hexa Climate Solutions for up to 83 MW of solar for phase 1 of its 350 MW AI-ready Navi Mumbai campus. Delivery starts December 2026. Digital Edge describes 83 MW as the maximum renewable capacity permitted under current regulations for that phase. — [Digital Edge press release](https://www.digitaledgedc.com/resources/newsroom/digital-edge-india-83mw-solar-ppa-recycled-water-data-center/)
- STT GDC India and CleanMax signed a 21 MWp solar PPA for Chennai data centres (April 2026). — [SolarQuarter](https://solarquarter.com/2026/04/01/cleanmax-stt-gdc-sign-21-mwp-solar-ppa-for-chennai-data-centres/)
- Equinix and CleanMax signed a 33 MW captive hybrid PPA (26.4 MWp solar plus 6.6 MW wind) for Equinix's Mumbai data centres, with operation phased in 2025. — [Stellarix](https://stellarix.com/article/india-data-center-boom-renewable-energy-digital-growth/) (secondary)
- CleanMax will develop 837 MW of solar and wind in Rajasthan and Karnataka, with Meta buying all the environmental attributes. — [IndexBox](https://www.indexbox.io/blog/india-data-center-boom-drives-renewable-energy-demand-beyond-supply/) (secondary, recycled from a Bloomberg/ET report)
- In November 2025, CtrlS signed an MoU with NTPC Green Energy for a renewable project of about 2 GW. — [MVA Pulse](https://www.mvapulse.com/india-data-center-power-demand-drives-rtc-renewable-growth/) (secondary)
- Renewable capacity needed for Indian data-center demand is projected to roughly double, from 16.1 GW to as much as 32.4 GW by 2030. — [IndexBox](https://www.indexbox.io/blog/india-data-center-boom-drives-renewable-energy-demand-beyond-supply/) (secondary)
- Industry commentary says plain solar or wind cannot meet 99.99% uptime needs, so solar-plus-storage and hybrid solar-wind (RTC) stacks are becoming the preferred structure. — [MVA Pulse](https://www.mvapulse.com/india-data-center-power-demand-drives-rtc-renewable-growth/)
- Indian data-center demand forecasts disagree. The CEA projects about 26 GW by 2036. Installed capacity was about 1.57 GW as of August 2026, with about 8 GW projected by 2030. Another estimate gives a 5 GW base case and 8–10 GW in an AI-accelerated case. — [Whalesbook (CEA)](https://www.whalesbook.com/news/English/energy/India-Power-Needs-for-Data-Centers-to-Reach-26-GW-by-2036/6ad0d91279a16deaeaf305dc); [Earthsync](https://earthsync.io/resources/india-data-centres-anchor-load); [Invest India whitepaper, Oct 2026](https://static.investindia.gov.in/s3fs-public/2026-10/cw_x_invest_india_whitepaper_.pdf)

### Inferences
- Hyperscalers are now the clean-PPA market. When non-tech buyers pull back, the four of them set prices and volumes, especially in the U.S.
- In India, regulatory caps on open-access or captive capacity per phase (as the Digital Edge note implies) appear to limit how much renewable power a data center can contract directly. Storage-backed RTC/FDRE is the natural answer to the firmness problem, so BESS is the key enabler.
- The Google CFE figures show that hourly matching is hardest in Asia (Singapore, Japan, Taiwan). Those are exactly the markets where firm clean supply such as RTC hybrids, nuclear and storage commands a premium.

### Gaps
- I found no primary source for 2025 BNEF contracted volumes for Google or Microsoft individually.
- I could not confirm Google's 2024 hourly CFE figure (often quoted as 66%, from the 2025 Environmental Report). The latest I verified is 64% for 2023.
- I found no primary-sourced RTC/FDRE PPAs between data-center developers and ReNew, Adani Green, Tata Power Renewables or AM Green. Claims that AWS Hyderabad uses ReNew power and AdaniConneX Chennai is 100% green via Adani Green come only from low-quality blogs. Tariffs (INR/kWh) for RTC/FDRE deals were not found.
- Green open access rules (the 100 kW threshold under the GEOA Rules 2022), state DC policies (Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, UP, Telangana) and banking provisions were not researched for lack of tool budget.

## 2. BESS for data centers: diesel replacement, grid flexibility, large solar+storage, xAI Megapacks, economics

### Takeaway
BESS plays three roles for data centers: (a) on-site backup or UPS that partly displaces diesel, demonstrated at MWh scale (Microsoft Sweden 16 MWh; Google Belgium about 2.5–5.5 MWh); (b) enabling flexible or curtailable load and faster grid connection; and (c) GWh-scale firming of co-located or dedicated solar (Masdar/EWEC 5.2 GW PV plus 19 GWh delivering 1 GW baseload; Google/Intersect Quantum in Texas, 840 MW PV plus 1.3 GWh). Cost trends now favour storage over peaking gas.

### Cited Findings
**Masdar/EWEC RTC project (Abu Dhabi)**
- The project pairs 5.2 GW (DC) of solar PV with a 19 GWh BESS and is designed to deliver up to 1 GW of baseload power around the clock. It is billed as the world's first gigawatt-scale RTC renewables project. — [pv magazine](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2025/10/24/masdar-ewec-break-ground-on-1-gw-baseload-solar-plus-storage-project/); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/government/uae-s-first-round-the-clock-facility-to-produce-1gw-clean-energy-1.500015796)
- The project was announced in January 2025 and broke ground in October 2025, targeting operation in 2027. Cost is reported at more than AED 22 billion (about $6 billion), and another report gives $6.1 billion with $1 billion of Masdar equity. More than 10,000 jobs are expected. — [ESG News](https://esgnews.com/?p=39589); [Engineering News](https://engineeringnews.co.za/article/round-the-clock-renewable-energy-project-united-arab-emirates-2026-10-23)
- Reported suppliers are CATL (BESS), Jinko/JA Solar (modules) and POWERCHINA and L&T (EPC). This comes from one analysis and is unverified. — [Interesting Engineering](https://interestingengineering.com/energy/uae-launches-world-first-24-7-renewable-energy-project)
- EWEC's CEO framed the project as supporting critical industries including AI. No named data-center offtaker has been disclosed. — [pv magazine](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2025/10/24/masdar-ewec-break-ground-on-1-gw-baseload-solar-plus-storage-project/)

**Google / Intersect Power**
- Google agreed in December 2025 to buy Intersect for $4.75 billion. The deal closed in March 2026, with Google buying TPG Rise Climate's stake. Google acquired the in-development and under-construction assets plus the "digital power" (data-center co-location) assets. — [Solar Power World](https://www.solarpowerworldonline.com/2025/12/google-buys-renewable-energy-project-developer-intersect-for-4-75-billion/); [PV Tech](https://www.pv-tech.org/google-finalises-intersect-acquisition-to-bring-data-centre-power-in-house/); [DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/googles-475bn-acquisition-of-intersect-power-closes/)
- The first co-located data center and power site is under construction in Haskell County, Texas ("Quantum"), with a reported 840 MW of solar PV and 1.3 GWh of BESS. — [Energy-Storage.News](https://www.energy-storage.news/google-acquires-data-centre-and-energy-developer-intersect-for-us4-75-billion/); [Industrial Info](https://www.industrialinfo.com/iirenergy/industry-news/article/google-acquires-intersect-power-specializing-in-large-solar-plants-bess--351295)
- The grid-tied business was spun off as a separate company (IPX). It holds 4.4 GW of solar PV and 8.8 GWh of BESS in construction or operation, mainly in Texas and California, and Google remains an investor. — [PV Tech](https://www.pv-tech.org/google-finalises-intersect-acquisition-to-bring-data-centre-power-in-house/)

**Backup and diesel replacement**
- Microsoft deployed a 16 MWh BESS (24 MW peak) at a Swedish data center to replace a bank of diesel generators, providing about 80 minutes of full backup. Saft describes this kind of design as a grid-forming microgrid with blackstart capability. — [Saft](https://saft.com/en/market-sectors/energy-storage/data-centers); [DCD analysis](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/analysis/watts-next-how-can-batteries-be-best-utilized-in-data-center/)
- Google has run a lithium-ion battery at its St. Ghislain, Belgium data center since 2020. One source says it is about 2.5 MWh and another says 2.75 MW/5.5 MWh, so the figures conflict. — [DCD analysis](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/analysis/watts-next-how-can-batteries-be-best-utilized-in-data-center/); [howtostoreelectricity.com](https://howtostoreelectricity.com/data-center-backup-power-bess-vs-diesel/)
- The economic case is stacking: the same battery shaves demand charges, captures arbitrage and earns frequency-regulation or demand-response revenue on days it is not needed for backup. — [Saft](https://saft.com/en/media-resources/our-stories/energy-storage-data-centers-how-combine-resilience-and-roi); [DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/analysis/watts-next-how-can-batteries-be-best-utilized-in-data-center/)
- Peer-reviewed or academic work surveys renewable and battery alternatives to diesel backup for data centers. — [ResearchGate: Moving Beyond Diesel Generators](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/387214764_Moving_Beyond_Diesel_Generators_Exploring_Renewable_Backup_Alternatives_for_Data_Centers)

**Grid flexibility deals (Google)**
- In August 2025, Google signed demand-response agreements with Indiana Michigan Power (I&M) and TVA. These are its first DR deals targeting machine-learning workloads. They build on a 2024 pilot with Omaha Public Power District, where Google cut ML load during three grid events. — [DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/google-partners-with-im-and-tva-to-expand-use-of-demand-response-at-its-ai-data-centers/); [Latitude Media](https://www.latitudemedia.com/news/google-expands-demand-response-to-target-machine-learning-workloads/)
- The I&M deal covers the Fort Wayne, Indiana data center. It is paired with a "Clean Capacity Arrangement" under which Google transfers accredited clean capacity to I&M. I&M discounts Google's rates when Google curtails, and says the arrangement avoids grid upgrades that would otherwise fall on other ratepayers. The Indiana Utility Regulatory Commission has approved it. — [DCD (IURC approval)](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/google-and-im-gain-regulatory-approval-for-demand-response-system-at-fort-wayne-data-center-in-indiana/); [Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/national/data-center/google-data-centers-to-curb-power-use-at-utilities-behest-bringing-demand-response-closer-to-mainstream-130463)
- Neither the financial terms nor the TVA site have been disclosed. — [Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/national/data-center/google-data-centers-to-curb-power-use-at-utilities-behest-bringing-demand-response-closer-to-mainstream-130463)

**xAI**
- Colossus 2 is reported as powered mainly by Southaven gas turbines, with plans to scale through a Solaris partnership by Q2 2027, supported by grid connections and Tesla Megapacks. This comes from a directory listing only. — [Baxtel](https://baxtel.com/data-center/xai-macrohard)

**Economics**
- An October 2026 analysis finds 4-hour battery storage is now cheaper to install than gas turbines globally. It puts 4-hour BESS at about $120/MWh in 2026 and GCC single-axis-tracker solar LCOE at about $37/MWh, falling toward $24/MWh by 2035. — [Solar Power World](https://www.solarpowerworldonline.com/2026/10/4-hour-battery-storage-is-cheaper-to-install-than-gas-turbines-all-across-globe/)

### Inferences
- The Masdar/EWEC ratio (about 5.2x PV DC overbuild and about 19 hours of storage per GW of firm output) is a useful rule of thumb for what "solar-only firm 24/7" requires in high-irradiance regions. It is the template for GCC and Indian RTC offers to data centers.
- Google buying Intersect, rather than signing PPAs, signals a shift to vertical integration ("bring your own power"). Co-located solar plus BESS sited with the data-center campus avoids interconnection queues.
- The diesel replacement case is strongest where batteries also earn grid revenue. Full replacement for multi-day outages is not yet shown, so batteries mostly cover the first minutes to hours, with diesel or gas behind them.

### Gaps
- I found no verified MW/MWh data for xAI's Megapack deployment at Colossus 1 (widely reported as about 150 MW of Megapacks, but unverified here).
- I found no LCOS or $/kWh capex figures specific to data-center BESS, and no India- or GCC-specific BESS tender prices (e.g., SECI or NHPC INR/MW/month BESS tariffs).
- I could not confirm the claim that Microsoft will phase out diesel by 2030.

## 3. On-site gas turbines and fuel cells (Bloom), behind-the-meter gas campuses, emissions and permitting

### Takeaway
Behind-the-meter gas is the fastest "speed-to-power" route for AI campuses, but it is drawing serious permitting and environmental-justice backlash (xAI in Memphis and Southaven). Fuel cells (Bloom SOFC) are scaling through a Brookfield deal of up to $5 billion, though they run mainly on natural gas.

### Cited Findings
- xAI admitted in January 2026 to running 27 unpermitted turbines. Later reporting counts 59 unpermitted units (57 at Southaven, Mississippi, plus 2 elsewhere). — [TheNextWeb](https://thenextweb.com/news/xai-59-unpermitted-gas-turbines-memphis-southaven)
- In 2025, Mississippi regulators let xAI run turbines without an air permit for up to 12 months. A March 2026 permit covers 41 permanent turbines at Southaven, issued after one public hearing. — [512 Pixels](https://512pixels.net/?p=33290); [TheNextWeb](https://thenextweb.com/news/xai-59-unpermitted-gas-turbines-memphis-southaven)
- Southaven air modeling projects about 423.4 tons/year of NOx, a potential Clean Air Act major source. SELC cited manufacturer data suggesting 1,200–2,000 tons/year of NOx. — [512 Pixels](https://512pixels.net/?p=33290); [NBC](https://www.nbcsandiego.com/news/business/money-report/elon-musks-xai-is-polluting-air-in-memphis-using-more-gas-turbines-than-permitted-advocacy-group-says/3801306/?amp=1)
- Shelby County permits cover 15 turbines at the Memphis site, with fines of up to $10,000 per violation. — [NBC](https://www.nbcsandiego.com/news/business/money-report/musks-xai-scores-permit-for-gas-burning-turbines-to-power-grok-supercomputer-in-memphis/3861835)
- In January 2026, EPA said such generators are not exempt from permitting, but it is now weighing "regulatory flexibilities" for portable units. The NAACP and SELC (with Earthjustice) sued in April 2026, and DOJ intervened on xAI's side in June, citing national security. — [TheNextWeb](https://thenextweb.com/news/xai-59-unpermitted-gas-turbines-memphis-southaven); [UNC Journal of Civil Rights Law](https://journals.law.unc.edu/nccivilrightslaw/2026/05/musk-mucking-up-memphis/)
- In October 2025, Brookfield committed up to $5 billion to deploy Bloom SOFCs at AI data centers worldwide, with a European site to follow. Bloom's stock rose 24.5% to $108.17. — [DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/bloom-energy-signs-5bn-partnership-with-brookfield-to-deploy-fuel-cell-tech-across-ai-data-centers/); [ESG Today](https://www.esgtoday.com/brookfield-bloom-energy-sign-5-billion-deal-to-power-ai-data-centers-with-fuel-cells/)
- Bloom has "hundreds of MW" deployed with AEP, Equinix and Oracle, and planned to double manufacturing capacity from 1 GW to 2 GW per year by end-2026 (about $100 million capex). A hyperscaler cancelled a Bloom contract for three Oregon data centers the prior year. The cells run mainly on natural gas. — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/brookfield-backs-bloom-energy-5-220121193.html); [Hydronews](https://www.hydronews.it/en/bloom-energys-fuel-cell-systems-increasingly-crucial-in-the-usa-network-and-not-just-data-centres-5-billion-agreement-with-brookfield/?amp=1)

### Inferences
- Gas BTM buys speed but brings carbon, NOx and permitting risk. This creates an opening for solar plus BESS hybrids that cut gas run-hours, and for storage that firms gas-backed microgrids.
- Federal policy (DOJ and EPA flexibility) is currently tilting toward allowing fast BTM gas in the U.S., while local opposition rises.

### Gaps
- I found no cost data ($/MWh) for BTM gas turbines, reciprocating engines or Bloom fuel cells, and no terms for the AEP–Bloom or Oracle–Bloom deals (AEP's reported 1 GW Bloom order is unverified here).
- Other gas campuses (e.g., Homer City PA, Meta's Hyperion/Entergy gas plants in Louisiana, Crusoe/Stargate Abilene) were not researched.

## 4. Nuclear: restarts, SMR deals, timelines and realism; India SHANTI Act and Bharat Small Reactors

### Takeaway
Restarts are the only near-term nuclear supply, and even they slip: Crane (TMI-1) is targeted for 2H 2027, and Palisades has been delayed indefinitely after an August 2026 fuel-loading incident. SMR deals are small and late: Google–Kairos's first PPA is just 50 MW (Hermes 2, about 2030), within a 500 MW-by-2035 framework. India's SHANTI Act (December 2025) opens nuclear to private and foreign players, but BSR commercialization is still at the RFP and business-model stage.

### Cited Findings
- **Crane (TMI-1), Constellation/Microsoft:** Microsoft holds a 20-year PPA. The restart was pulled forward from 2028 to 2H 2027. A $1.0 billion DOE loan closed in November 2025. A Reuters report said full grid deliverability might not come until 2031 because PJM transmission upgrades may run past 2030. Constellation says that date concerns capacity deliverability, not the restart itself. The NRC issued its final environmental assessment and FONSI in September 2026. — [Constellation](https://www.constellationenergy.com/news/2025/09/one-year-later-crane-clean-energy-center-still-in-the-spotlight-and-ahead-of-schedule.html); [POWER](https://www.powermag.com/constellation-races-to-revive-crane-nuclear-plant-amid-tight-timelines-market-shifts/); [World Nuclear News](https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/crane-clean-energy-centre-on-line-for-ahead-of-schedule-restart); [Local21](https://local21news.com/news/local/2027-restart-of-nuclear-reactor-grid-at-the-crane-still-on-track-three-mile-island-nuclear-power-crane-clean-energy-pa-pennsylvania)
- **Palisades (Holtec, 800 MWe PWR, Michigan):** The plant transitioned to operational licensing status on 25 August 2025. The restart slipped to "early 2026", then to possibly late March. Primary-system passivation was completed by April 2026. The restart was then delayed indefinitely after a 30 August 2026 fuel-loading incident involving a partially used fuel assembly. — [Michigan Public](https://www.michiganpublic.org/environment-climate-change/2025-12-17/palisades-nuclear-plant-restart-plans-pushed-back-to-early-2026); [ANS](https://www.ans.org/news/2026-04-02/article-7901/holtec-hits-milestones-in-palisades-restart-new-reactor-projects/); [NEI Magazine](https://www.neimagazine.com/news/palisades-restart-delayed/)
- **Google–Kairos–TVA:** Google's framework covers up to 500 MW across 6–7 reactors by 2035. The first PPA is TVA buying up to 50 MW from Hermes 2 (Oak Ridge, Tennessee) for Google data centers in Tennessee and Alabama. Hermes 2 has been scaled to one reactor of up to 50 MWe, targeting about 2030. The NRC construction permit was issued in November 2024 and ground was broken in April 2026. An operating licence is still needed. — [World Nuclear News](https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/kairos-breaks-ground-for-hermes-2-reactor); [DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/tva-signs-50mw-ppa-with-smr-developer-kairos-for-google-data-centers-in-tennessee-and-alabama/); [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/08/18/google-kairos-nuclear-smr-tennessee-valley-authority-tva-data-center-ai.html)
- **Meta and Amazon:** Their 2025 contracting included 4.7 GW of nuclear combined (per BNEF). — [ESG Today](https://www.esgtoday.com/amazon-meta-google-microsoft-account-for-half-of-global-clean-energy-purchase-deals-in-2025-report/)
- **India SHANTI Act:** The Lok Sabha passed it on 17 December 2025, the Rajya Sabha on 18 December, and it received assent on 20 December 2025. It replaces the Atomic Energy Act 1962 and the CLND Act 2010. It lets Indian private firms, JVs and foreign entities build, own and operate plants, ending NPCIL's monopoly. Operator liability is capped by plant capacity and supplier liability is removed. One source reports a 49% FDI cap, which is unconfirmed. — [IDSA issue brief (Feb 2026)](https://idsa.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Issue-Brief-Niranjan-C-Oak-Bhawna-Budhwar-17-Feb-2026.pdf); [Drishti IAS](https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-analysis/shanti-bill-2025/print_manually); [PIB](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/dec/doc20251219739001.pdf); [Rauias](https://compass.rauias.com/current-affairs/shanti-bill-2025-india-nuclear-energy-sector)
- **Bharat Small Reactors:** NPCIL's RFP for a fleet of 220 MW BSRs, financed and built by industry, had its deadline extended to 31 March 2026. Interested firms include Adani, Reliance, Tata, Jindal and JSW. NPCIL held a January 2026 workshop to rework the BSR business model under the new law. — [World Nuclear News](https://world-nuclear-news.org/articles/indias-shanti-bill-completes-legislative-process); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/latest/economy/story/shanti-act-npcil-to-rework-nuclear-business-model-in-light-of-new-law-510177-2026-01-09)
- Indian policy framing positions nuclear as baseload for AI, data, semiconductors and research. No India nuclear-for-data-center deal was found. — [IDSA](https://idsa.in/publisher/issuebrief/shanti-act-and-indias-nuclear-energy-governance-framework)

### Inferences
- Realistic nuclear contribution to data-center supply before 2030 is limited to restarts (about 0.8–0.9 GW each, with slippage risk) and uprates or PPAs on existing plants. SMRs are a 2030–2035+ option at tens to hundreds of MW per site.
- Solar plus BESS and gas fill the 2025–2032 gap. Even nuclear-anchored campuses will likely need storage and flexible load in the interim.
- In India, SHANTI makes captive or private nuclear for data-center parks legally possible, but first private BSR power is unlikely before the early 2030s.

### Gaps
- The Amazon–X-energy deal (Xe-100, an often-cited 5 GW by 2039 target, Energy Northwest Cascade project) and Oracle's announced SMR-powered campus were not verified in this session. Do not cite figures without checking.
- I found no outcome of NPCIL's BSR RFP after March 2026, and no confirmation of the 49% FDI cap.
- I found no LCOE or PPA price data for restarts or SMRs.

## 5. Co-location at power plants, FERC rulings (Talen–Amazon Susquehanna), and flexible-load research (Duke)

### Takeaway
FERC's November 2024 rejection of expanded behind-the-meter co-location at Susquehanna (300 to 480 MW) pushed Talen and Amazon into a 1.92 GW front-of-the-meter PPA (June 2025). The Duke Nicholas Institute study shows 76 GW (0.25% curtailment), 98 GW (0.5%) and 126 GW (1%) of new load could be absorbed if loads are flexible. Battery storage is a natural tool for supplying that flexibility.

### Cited Findings
- On 1 November 2024, FERC voted 2–1 to reject PJM's amended ISA, which would have raised co-located load at Susquehanna from 300 MW to 480 MW. Christie and See formed the majority and Chairman Phillips dissented. — [POWER](https://www.powermag.com/ferc-blocks-pjm-proposal-to-expand-amazon-data-center-load-at-susquehanna-nuclear-plant/); [World Nuclear News](https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/regulator-rejects-amended-susquehanna-power-agreement); [Talen 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1622536/000162828024044828/a20241103pressreleaseferco.htm)
- FERC's December 2024 order (reaffirmed in April 2025) said it would address rehearing later. Talen appealed to the Fifth Circuit, and the case was transferred to the Third Circuit in November 2025. — [Talen FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1622536/000162253626000017/tln-20251231.htm)
- In June 2025, Talen and AWS signed a 1,920 MW PPA from Susquehanna. The existing 300 MW co-location shifts to a front-of-the-meter structure that does not require FERC approval. — [RTO Insider](https://www.rtoinsider.com/107790-talen-amazon-ppa-power-susquehanna-energy/); [JEPIC](https://www.jepic-usa.org/digests/2025/6/12/usa-talen-to-sell-19gw-from-susquehanna-plant-to-amazon)
- **Duke "Rethinking Load Growth" (February 2025):** The study covers 22 balancing authorities, about 95% of U.S. load. It finds 76 GW of new load (about 10% of aggregate peak) can be integrated at 0.25% average annual curtailment, 98 GW at 0.5% and 126 GW at 1.0%. — [Duke Nicholas Institute](https://nicholasinstitute.duke.edu/articles/three-key-takeaways-rethinking-load-growth-us-power-systems); [S&P Global](https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/articles/2025/2/us-grid-could-serve-100-gw-of-added-load-through-modest-curtailments-study-87513622)
- At 0.5% curtailment, the leading regions are PJM (18 GW), MISO (15 GW), ERCOT (10 GW), SPP (10 GW) and Southern Co (8 GW). The study assumes an average curtailment event lasts about 2 hours, consistent with short-duration batteries. — [Renewable Energy World](https://www.renewableenergyworld.com/power-grid/significant-flexible-load-potential-for-us-grid-finds-duke-study); [Facilities Dive](https://www.facilitiesdive.com/news/us-grid-headroom-flexible-load-data-center-ai-ev-duke-report/739938/)
- A critique argues that the system-level headroom figure is being misused to justify reduced network-upgrade obligations for individual data centers. This is a single-author opinion. — [Medium (B. Handshy)](https://medium.com/@brianwhandshy/dukes-rethinking-load-growth-study-is-being-misused-in-the-interconnection-debate-b7d3d981e8ca)

### Inferences
- The FERC outcome made front-of-meter "virtual co-location" PPAs the default structure at existing nuclear plants, preserving grid charges and capacity-market contributions.
- Duke's two-hour average curtailment event maps directly onto 2–4 hour BESS. A data center with on-site batteries can be grid-flexible without curtailing compute, which is a concrete value proposition for storage (faster interconnection in exchange for flexibility).

### Gaps
- PJM's co-location tariff proceedings and FERC's 2025–2026 large-load or co-location orders (e.g., FERC's December 2025 directive to PJM on co-location rules) were not verified here. The Talen–Amazon deal value (widely reported as about $18 billion) was not verified.

## 6. India and GCC specifics: green open access, state RE policies, GCC solar costs, hydrogen

### Takeaway
The GCC has the world's cheapest solar (about 1.04–1.09 US¢/kWh in Saudi Arabia; 1.35 ¢/kWh at Al Dhafra in 2020) and is building the first GW-scale 24/7 solar plus BESS plant. That makes it a natural "solar+storage-powered AI hub". India's levers are captive and green open access plus hybrid RTC procurement, but per-project regulatory caps and firmness constrain direct sourcing.

### Cited Findings
- Saudi Arabia: the 1.4 GW Najran solar project won a tariff of 1.09 US¢/kWh, and the Kingdom had tendered 64 GW of renewables by end-2025. — [SolarQuarter (Jan 2026)](https://solarquarter.com/2026/01/06/saudi-arabia-accelerates-renewable-energy-growth-with-64-gw-tendered-and-record-low-power-costs/)
- Saudi Arabia: the 600 MW Shuaibah project was reported at about 1.04 ¢/kWh ($10.40/MWh), an earlier world record. — [RenewEconomy](https://reneweconomy.com.au/saudi-solar-plant-locks-in-new-record-low-price-for-power-1-04c-kwh/)
- UAE: Al Dhafra (2 GW) was set at 1.35 ¢/kWh in 2020, and DEWA's MBR Solar Park Phase 5 PPA was 1.6953 ¢/kWh. — [POWER](https://www.powermag.com/record-low-solar-prices-drive-middle-east-projects/)
- GCC solar LCOE is about $37/MWh (single-axis tracker) and is projected to fall toward $24/MWh by 2035. Saudi Arabia and the UAE are projected below $20/MWh by about 2033. 4-hour BESS costs about $120/MWh in 2026. — [Solar Power World (Oct 2026)](https://www.solarpowerworldonline.com/2026/10/4-hour-battery-storage-is-cheaper-to-install-than-gas-turbines-all-across-globe/)
- UAE's Masdar/EWEC 1 GW 24/7 project (5.2 GW PV plus 19 GWh, about $6 billion, target 2027) is positioned explicitly to serve AI and advanced industries. — [pv magazine](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2025/10/24/masdar-ewec-break-ground-on-1-gw-baseload-solar-plus-storage-project/)
- India: Digital Edge's 83 MW solar PPA was the "maximum renewable capacity permissible under current regulations" for phase 1 of its 350 MW Navi Mumbai campus. — [Digital Edge](https://www.digitaledgedc.com/resources/newsroom/digital-edge-india-83mw-solar-ppa-recycled-water-data-center/)
- India: Invest India and Cushman & Wakefield published an October 2026 whitepaper on data-center power infrastructure and the green transition. — [Invest India whitepaper](https://static.investindia.gov.in/s3fs-public/2026-10/cw_x_invest_india_whitepaper_.pdf)

### Inferences
- Implied GCC economics: about $10–37/MWh for PV energy plus about $120/MWh for 4-hour storage. A blended RTC solar plus BESS supply could plausibly undercut many grid tariffs for AI campuses. The Masdar/EWEC project's $6 billion for 1 GW firm output (about $6/W firm) is the key benchmark. This is my inference, not a sourced tariff.
- In India, phase-wise regulatory caps and the need for 24/7 supply make storage-backed hybrid (FDRE/RTC) PPAs and on-site BESS the main growth path for solar in data-center supply.

### Gaps

No sourced findings exist for hydrogen.
- I found no named GCC data-center PPA tariff (e.g., the Stargate UAE / G42 5 GW AI campus power supply, Saudi HUMAIN / DataVolt energy deals).
- The Najran 1.09 ¢/kWh figure comes from a trade site and should ideally be confirmed against SPPC or the Ministry of Energy.
