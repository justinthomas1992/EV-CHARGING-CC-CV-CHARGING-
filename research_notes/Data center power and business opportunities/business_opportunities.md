## 1. Data center build economics: capex per MW, electrical/power share, and equipment OEM growth and backlogs

### Takeaway
Non-IT build cost ("shell and core" plus MEP) is about **$10-11M per MW of IT load** globally. Electrical and mechanical systems take the majority of that, so roughly 50-80% of non-IT capex is MEP (the share depends on how it is counted). AI fit-out adds about $25M+/MW of IT equipment on top. Equipment OEMs are capacity-constrained and hold record backlogs: GE Vernova $176B, Siemens Energy Grid €51B, Vertiv $15B at end-2025. Electrical scope is therefore the most bankable "picks and shovels" layer.

### Cited Findings
- **[Estimate] JLL 2026 Global Data Center Outlook:** average global shell-and-core construction cost of **$10.7M per MW in 2025**. This excludes servers and networking and covers the envelope, electrical distribution and mechanical systems — [ULE Group summary of JLL/CSIS](https://ulegroup.com/blogs/articles/data-center-power-consumption-construction-cost-report-2026)
- **[Estimate] CSIS:** non-IT infrastructure costs about **$10M/MW** as a US baseline — [ULE Group](https://ulegroup.com/blogs/articles/data-center-power-consumption-construction-cost-report-2026)
- **[Estimate] JLL:** AI tenants can spend **up to $25M per MW** on GPUs, networking and related IT on top of the base build — [ULE Group](https://ulegroup.com/blogs/articles/data-center-power-consumption-construction-cost-report-2026)
- **[Estimate] Bernstein, all-in AI capacity:** about **$35B per GW** (≈$35M/MW). GPUs are 39% of total capex. Within it: generators/turbines ~6%, transformers ~5%, UPS ~4%, thermal management (air plus liquid) ~4% — [Investing.com, "How much does a GW of data center capacity actually cost"](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/how-much-does-a-gw-of-data-center-capacity-actually-cost-4314046)

- **[Estimate] MEP share:** mechanical, electrical and plumbing can take **up to ~50% of total budgets**. - **[Reported] Vertiv:** Q4 2025 backlog **$15.0B, +109% y/y**, book-to-bill about **2.9x**. Vertiv has said it will **stop reporting quarterly orders and backlog**. - **[Reported] Schneider Electric:** quarterly revenue **€11.46B, +17.9% (organic +16.9%)**. - **[Reported] Eaton:** record Q3 2025 results, with accelerating orders and backlog growth driven by data center, utility and aerospace. No 2026 figure was found — [Electrical Trends](https://electricaltrends.com/?p=3697806)

### Inferences
- [Inference] On a ~$10.7M/MW non-IT budget with ~50-60% MEP, the electrical-plus-mechanical scope is about **$5-7M per MW**. Applying Bernstein's percentages to $35M/MW gives generators/turbines ~$2.1M/MW, transformers ~$1.75M/MW and UPS ~$1.4M/MW, about **$5M+/MW of power-train equipment** per AI MW. For each 1 GW of AI capacity, roughly **$5B of power-equipment demand** sits outside the IT spend.
- [Inference] OEM backlogs (GE Vernova, Siemens Energy) run 2-4 years. Transformer and turbine **slot reservations** are therefore a competitive asset: developers and EPCs that hold frame agreements can win projects on schedule certainty more than on price.
- [Inference] Schneider's strongest lines (prefab systems, UPS, cooling) and Vertiv's revenue slippage on "multiphase execution" suggest that **installation, integration and commissioning capacity**, not only manufacturing, is a bottleneck. That is an opening for EPC and testing firms.

### Gaps

- ABB Electrification data center order growth for 2026 was not found. Eaton 2026 backlog was not found.
- No reliable split of capex/MW between shell, electrical and mechanical from a single primary source. Estimates use different scopes and are not directly comparable.
- No India-specific capex/MW from a primary source. Industry commentary often cites a ₹40-60 crore/MW (~$5-7M/MW) range, but I did not verify this in this session.

## 2. IPP opportunities: hyperscaler PPAs, firm/RTC power, powered land and energy parks, co-located solar+BESS

### Takeaway
Hyperscalers now buy **generation developers outright** (Google-Intersect, $4.75B) and the new "energy park" format co-locates GW-scale solar, BESS and data centers. PPA prices are rising (~9% y/y in 2025). Hyperscalers sign 15-25-year contracts at premium pricing, though no published $/MWh figure isolates a "24/7 premium". In the GCC, Abu Dhabi's **5.2 GW solar + 19 GWh BESS → 1 GW baseload** project (~$6B) sets the template for firm renewable power for AI campuses. In India, the market is still mostly 20-200 MW solar/hybrid captive and group-captive PPAs with colocation operators.

### Cited Findings
- **[Reported] Google-Intersect Power:** Alphabet agreed in Dec 2025 to buy Intersect for about **$4.75B cash plus assumed debt**, and closed by March 2026. Intersect will focus on "**energy parks**" that co-locate hyperscale data centers with generation and storage. The flagship is the **Quantum project in Haskell County, Texas: 640 MW solar + 1.3 GWh BESS** built alongside a new data center campus. The grid-tied business was spun off as **IPX Power**, an independent IPP for co-located solar+BESS in Texas and California. pv magazine calls this the first time Big Tech bought a major renewable developer outright — [pv magazine](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/01/05/google-acquires-intersect-power-for-nearly-5-billion/); [Energy-Storage.news](https://www.energy-storage.news/?p=97111); [pv magazine USA](https://pv-magazine-usa.com/2026/01/02/google-acquires-clean-energy-developer-intersect-power-for-nearly-5-billion/)
- **[Estimate] PPA pricing:** solar and wind PPA prices rose about **9% in 2025 vs 2024**, driven by data center demand, supply chain costs and interest rates. LevelTen's Q4 2025 P25 solar price was about **$61-62/MWh** (+3.2% q/q, ~+9% y/y), per a secondary blog — [Global Data Center Hub](https://www.globaldatacenterhub.com/p/paper-megawatts-the-hidden-crisis); [CohnReznick, "Hyperscaler premium in renewable project valuation"](https://www.cohnreznick.com/insights/the-hyperscaler-premium-in-renewable-project-valuation/)
- **[Estimate] Contract tenor and premium:** Microsoft, Google, Amazon and Meta sign **15-25-year contracts at scale, often at premium pricing**, which raises project valuations. CohnReznick gives no percentage. Nuclear PPAs are reportedly **$80-110/MWh** (unverified, low-quality source) — [CohnReznick](https://www.cohnreznick.com/insights/the-hyperscaler-premium-in-renewable-project-valuation/); [IB Interview Questions](https://ibinterviewquestions.com/guides/energy-investment-banking/power-purchase-agreements-structure-pricing-bankability)
- **[Reported] 24/7 CFE:** Google targets round-the-clock carbon-free energy on every grid by 2030. Its 478 MW Dutch offshore deal gives about **90% hourly-matched** clean energy in the Netherlands — [McKinsey, "How hyperscalers are fueling the race for 24/7 clean power"](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/electric-power-and-natural-gas/our-insights/how-hyperscalers-are-fueling-the-race-for-24-7-clean-power)
- **[Reported] GCC firm solar:** Masdar and EWEC broke ground (Oct 2025) on **5.2 GW PV + 19 GWh BESS delivering 1 GW of 24/7 baseload**. COD target is **2027**, capex is **>AED 22B (~$6-6.1B)** including about $1B of Masdar equity, and it is expected to create 10,000+ jobs — [pv magazine](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2025/10/24/masdar-ewec-break-ground-on-1-gw-baseload-solar-plus-storage-project/); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/government/uae-s-first-round-the-clock-facility-to-produce-1gw-clean-energy-1.500015796); [Engineering News](https://engineeringnews.co.za/article/round-the-clock-renewable-energy-project-united-arab-emirates-2026-10-23)
- **[Reported] India colocation PPAs (2026):** **Digital Edge - Hexa Climate, 83 MW solar PPA** for phase 1 of a 350 MW Navi Mumbai campus. Delivery is phased from Dec 2026, and Digital Edge takes a **substantial minority equity stake** in the RE projects — [Energetica India](https://www.energetica-india.net/news/digital-edge-signs-83-mw-solar-ppa-with-hexa-climate-solutions-for-data-center-in-navi-mumbai)
- **[Reported]** **CleanMax - STT GDC, 21 MWp solar PPA** (Chennai, Apr 2026). **CleanMax - Iron Mountain** clean power (May 2026), framed around 24/7 CFE by 2040 — [SolarQuarter](https://solarquarter.com/2026/04/01/cleanmax-stt-gdc-sign-21-mwp-solar-ppa-for-chennai-data-centres); [Renewable Watch](https://renewablewatch.in/2026/05/07/cleanmax-to-supply-clean-energy-to-iron-mountains-data-centers-in-india/)
- **[Reported] Older reference:** Sify - Vibrant Energy 200 MW green PPA, dated Feb 2022 — [EPC World](https://www.epcworld.in/sify-signs-ppa-with-vibrant-energy-for-200-mw-of-green-power/)
- **[Reported] Adani-Google Vizag:** the ~$15B (2026-2030) Google AI hub includes **co-investment in new transmission lines, clean energy generation and energy storage in Andhra Pradesh** with AdaniConneX and Airtel — [DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/product-news/adani-and-google-partner-to-build-indias-largest-data-center-campus-in-visakhapatnam/); [Adani Connect](https://connect.adani.com/media/2025/oct25/connex/)
- **[Estimate] Valuation premium for contracted RE:** DC operators with **contracted renewable PPAs have traded at 20-24x EBITDA vs 14-16x** for grid-dependent operators (trade press; unverified) — [Datacentres.com](https://www.datacentres.com/news/data-centre-m-a-hits-87b-in-h1-2026-as-hyperscalers-reshape-portfolio-architectu-slot1-2026-08-04)

### Inferences
- [Inference] Masdar's ratio of **~5.2 MW PV + ~19 MWh BESS per MW of firm load** (about 6.6 MW of solar-plus-storage capacity and roughly $6M of capex per firm MW) is a useful sizing rule for **RTC solar+BESS for data centers** in high-irradiance markets (GCC, Rajasthan/Gujarat). For each GW of AI load served 24/7 from solar, roughly 5 GW of PV and 19 GWh of BESS must be built. That is a very large EPC and IPP pipeline.
- [Inference] In India, the IPP route to data centers runs mostly through **group-captive / open-access solar-wind-BESS hybrids** with equity participation by the offtaker (Digital Edge-Hexa model), because group-captive rules require consumer equity of ~26%. IPPs that can offer FDRE/RTC structures with storage are best placed. Policy waivers on transmission and wheeling (UP, see §4) improve the economics.
- [Inference] "Powered land" (sites with secured interconnection and co-located generation) is becoming a tradable asset class. Google bought Intersect partly for its site and interconnection pipeline. Indian and GCC developers holding land, transmission connectivity and RE capacity (Adani Green, AM Green, ReNew, ACWA Power, Masdar) can monetize them as data-center-ready parks.

### Gaps
- No sourced $/MWh premium for 24/7 or hourly-matched contracts versus annual-matched PPAs. LevelTen primary reports were not retrieved.
- No confirmed AM Green, Tata Power, ReNew or ACWA Power data center supply deals were found in this session. ACWA Power's role in Saudi data center power was not confirmed.
- Indian RTC/FDRE tariff levels (₹/kWh) for data center offtake were not retrieved.
- Reliance Jamnagar's power arrangement (captive solar + BESS from its giga-factories) was not confirmed. Only the reported "3 GW" campus size was found.

## 3. EPC opportunities: substations/transmission, MEP, BESS integration, prefab power skids, commissioning and testing

### Takeaway
EPC demand is concentrated in (a) **HV substations and transformers** (Siemens Energy Grid backlog €51B, driven by transformers), (b) **MEP**, which takes the majority of the ~$10.7M/MW non-IT budget, (c) **BESS on virtually every new project** according to a leading US electrical contractor, and (d) **prefab/modular power systems**, Schneider's fastest-growing line. Integration and multiphase commissioning are already causing OEM revenue slippage, which signals a skills and execution shortage.

### Cited Findings
- **[Reported/Estimate] BESS everywhere:** a **Rosendin executive said BESS is now on virtually every data center project Rosendin builds**. Traditional generator backup coverage has fallen to **15-40% of load on new projects** (ROTH Capital webinar, Jul 2026) — [ESS News](https://www.ess-news.com/2026/07/15/data-center-power-shifts-favor-bess-solid-state-transformers/); [pv magazine USA](https://pv-magazine-usa.com/2026/07/15/data-center-power-shifts-favor-bess-solid-state-transformers/)
- **[Reported]** **FlexGen + Rosendin "BESSUPS"** is an integrated BESS-as-UPS offering for data centers — [Data Center Frontier](https://www.datacenterfrontier.com/energy/article/55296732/transforming-data-center-energy-the-innovative-flexgen-and-rosendin-bessups-collaboration)
- **[Reported] Cummins (Aug 2026):** its largest BESS contract to date, a **5 MWh LFP system** for a US data center to manage AI load fluctuations and reduce diesel noise and emissions. **Frontier/Baker Hughes:** 16 gas turbines plus BESS plus synchronous condensers for several US data center projects, a diesel-free hybrid design — [Battery Tech Online](https://www.batterytechonline.com/stationary-batteries/cummins-wins-its-largest-bess-contract-for-us-data-center-project); [Data Center Knowledge](https://www.datacenterknowledge.com/energy-power-supply/battery-storage-gains-ground-as-data-centers-seek-diesel-alternatives)
- **[Reported] Policy driver:** Texas SB 6 encourages on-site dispatchable power for loads above 50 MVA — [Data Center Knowledge](https://www.datacenterknowledge.com/energy-power-supply/battery-storage-gains-ground-as-data-centers-seek-diesel-alternatives)
- **[Reported] Prefab:** Schneider's strongest data center demand is in **prefabricated data center systems**, cooling and three-phase UPS — [ARC Advisory](https://www.arcweb.com/blog/schneider-electric-q2-growth-highlights-data-center-automation-industrial-software-momentum)
- **[Reported] Execution bottleneck:** Vertiv attributed some Q2 2026 revenue slippage to "multiphase project execution and temporary supply chain congestion" — [Trefis](https://www.trefis.com/stock/vrt/articles/613345/the-vertiv-stock-signal-was-never-in-the-demand-headline/2026-08-27)
- **[Reported] Stargate UAE (Khazna/G42):** all long-lead equipment procurement is complete, first mechanical deliveries are on site, and about **3,500 workers** are on site (Oct 2025). The first **200 MW is targeted for 2026** within a 1 GW cluster of the 5 GW UAE-US AI Campus — [MEA Tech Watch](https://meatechwatch.com/2025/10/17/g42s-1gw-stargate-uae-ai-infrastructure-cluster-nears-2026-completion-milestone/); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/markets/stargate-uae-first-phase-of-200mw-to-complete-by-2026-1.500310364)
- **[Reported] Saudi EPC awards:** HUMAIN awarded **Al Moammar Information Systems** contracts for **200 MW** of new data center capacity (Aug 2026). HUMAIN-DataVolt started work at Oxagon/NEOM: 100 MW within a 360 MW phase 1 of a **1.5 GW** campus, first 100 MW in 2028 — [MEED](https://guest.meed.com/humain-and-datavolt-start-oxagon-data-centre-work/); [DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/saudi-arabias-humain-and-datavolt-collaborate-on-data-center-development/)

### Inferences
- [Inference] The EPC scope ladder, roughly from highest to lowest value per MW: HV grid connection (substation, lines, transformers), then MEP (switchgear, busway, UPS, cooling), then BESS/backup, then commissioning (Level 1-5 integrated systems testing). EPCs with **HV substation plus BESS plus MEP** capability can bid "**power island**" packages, with one wrap from grid tie to rack PDU.
- [Inference] In India, firms such as L&T, Tata Projects, Sterling and Wilson, KEC and Kalpataru (transmission), and solar EPCs moving into BESS, are positioned for this. Prefab skids (e-houses, modular substations, containerized BESS+UPS) shorten schedules and suit GCC desert sites with limited labor. This list is illustrative and was not verified for specific data center awards in this session.
- [Inference] Commissioning, QA/QC and Owner's Engineer services are scarce. Multi-phase AI campuses (200 MW phases) need repeated integrated systems testing, and OEMs are visibly constrained.

### Gaps
- No sourced market size for data center EPC or commissioning services, and no typical EPC margins. As general industry knowledge (not verified here), EPC EBITDA margins are typically mid-single to low-double digits.
- No India-specific data center substation/transmission EPC award values were retrieved.

## 4. Investor activity: major deals, India and GCC players, valuation and return metrics

### Takeaway
The largest data center deal on record, **AIP/MGX/GIP's $40B purchase of Aligned (closed 21 Jul 2026)**, shows that sovereign capital (MGX, KIA, Temasek) and infrastructure funds are paying platform prices for GW-scale pipelines. India has attracted about **$173B of cumulative DC investment commitments since 2021** (CBRE), with Adani and Reliance accounting for about 45% of announced investment. GCC programs (Stargate UAE 1 GW / 5 GW campus, HUMAIN targets of 1.9 GW by 2030 and ~6.6 GW by 2034) are state-led and backed by subsidized power. Multiples have compressed from their peak: listed operators trade at about 10.8x 2028E EBITDA (Needham), versus 18-20x for private AI-capable deals.

### Cited Findings
- **[Reported] Aligned Data Centers:** the AI Infrastructure Partnership (BlackRock, GIP, MGX, Microsoft, NVIDIA; backed by KIA and Temasek), MGX and GIP closed the **~$40B EV** acquisition of Aligned from Macquarie on **21 Jul 2026** (announced Oct 2025). It comes with **$5B of committed growth capital**. Portfolio: **51 campuses, >6.4 GW** operational and planned, implying roughly **$6M+ EV per MW of total pipeline** [Inference from reported numbers]. It is AIP's first investment. The prior record was Blackstone's AirTrunk deal (~$16.1-16.6B, 2024) — [WAM](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/c1c097j-aip-mgx-blackrocks-gip-close-40-billion); [DCD](https://datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/aligned-data-centers-sold-to-blackrock-and-mgx-in-record-breaking-40bn-deal); [Middle East AI News](https://www.middleeastainews.com/p/mgx-led-consortium-completes-40-billion)
- **[Reported] Meta Hyperion JV:** Meta 20%, **Blue Owl funds 80%**. The SPV "Beignet Investor" issued about **$27B of bonds due 2049 at 6.58%**, versus about 5.5% on comparable Meta corporate debt. ### Inferences
- [Inference] A $1.66M NOI per MW on an all-in landlord cost of about $10-14M/MW (powered shell plus MEP, excluding tenant IT) implies an **unlevered development yield of roughly 12-16%**, against exit cap rates of about 5-8%. That spread explains why platforms command roughly $6M+ EV per pipeline MW. It is a rough calculation from mixed sources and should be treated as indicative.
- [Inference] Power-side investors (IPPs, BESS funds) can capture part of this spread by owning the power asset behind the meter or in an adjacent park under 15-25-year hyperscaler contracts. Credit quality is investment-grade, but counterparty concentration is high.
- [Inference] India's capacity (1.6-2 GW) is small relative to its $173B of commitments. Only a fraction of announced capital will translate into built MW by 2030 (JLL: 6 GW needs $110B). Announcements should be discounted.

## 5. New business models: energy-as-a-service, BESS-as-a-service, waste heat, microgrids, flexibility, O&M/OE

### Takeaway
The most concrete emerging model is **BESS replacing or downsizing diesel backup** (generator coverage down to 15-40% on new US projects). These batteries can also provide grid services and absorb AI load swings. Hybrid on-site systems (gas turbines plus BESS plus synchronous condensers) and developer-owned energy parks (Intersect/IPX) are energy-as-a-service variants. No 2026 quantitative evidence was found on waste-heat reuse or flexibility revenues.

### Cited Findings
- **[Reported]** **Microsoft Sweden (Saft):** 4 × 4 MWh BESS gives up to 80 minutes of backup, replacing diesel, versus the conventional 48 hours of generator fuel (operating since June 2023) — [North American Clean Energy](https://www.nacleanenergy.com/energy-storage/saft-delivers-battery-energy-storage-system-bess-replacement-for-diesel-powered-backup-at-microsoft-data-center)
- **[Reported]** BESS is used to **manage AI-driven load fluctuations** (Cummins). Integrated **BESS-as-UPS** (FlexGen/Rosendin). Hybrid diesel-free turbine+BESS (Baker Hughes/Frontier) — [Battery Tech Online](https://www.batterytechonline.com/stationary-batteries/cummins-wins-its-largest-bess-contract-for-us-data-center-project); [Data Center Frontier](https://www.datacenterfrontier.com/energy/article/55296732/transforming-data-center-energy-the-innovative-flexgen-and-rosendin-bessups-collaboration)
- **[Estimate]** Shifting power architecture favors **BESS and solid-state transformers** — [ESS News](https://www.ess-news.com/2026/07/15/data-center-power-shifts-favor-bess-solid-state-transformers/)
- **[Reported]** Intersect/IPX: developer-owned co-located solar+BESS sells power to on-site data centers (the energy park model) — [Energy-Storage.news](https://www.energy-storage.news/?p=97111)
- **[Opinion]** BESS enables firmed RTC supply for Indian data centers — [pv magazine India](https://www.pv-magazine-india.com/2026/07/28/why-renewable-energy-will-power-indias-next-generation-of-data-centres/)
- **[Reported]** Digital Edge took minority equity in Hexa's RE projects, a co-ownership or captive energy-as-a-service structure — [Energetica India](https://www.energetica-india.net/news/digital-edge-signs-83-mw-solar-ppa-with-hexa-climate-solutions-for-data-center-in-navi-mumbai)

### Inferences
- [Inference] **BESS-as-a-service:** a third party owns the battery, provides backup and ride-through for an availability fee, and earns merchant or ancillary revenue (frequency response, arbitrage) when the battery is not needed for backup. This stacks revenue and lowers the data center's capex. In India it depends on regulation of behind-the-meter participation in ancillary markets. GCC markets are less liberalized, so availability-fee models are likelier there.
- [Inference] **Owner's Engineer, QA/QC and O&M:** the volume of multi-phase GW campuses (Stargate UAE, HUMAIN, Vizag, Jamnagar) and OEM execution constraints create demand for independent testing, grid-code compliance (BESS and synchronous condensers) and long-term O&M of power islands.
- [Inference] Waste-heat reuse is relevant mainly in cold-climate or district-heating markets (Nordics, Europe). Its relevance in India and the GCC is low, apart from absorption-chiller or desalination concepts.

## 6. Risks for investors and leaders

### Takeaway
The risks are real but not yet crystallized. Leverage is building in off-balance-sheet SPVs (Meta Hyperion at a ~110 bp premium over Meta's own debt) and at weaker credits (Oracle, with negative FCF and loans trading around 90 cents). Public DC multiples have already compressed 30-50%. For power-side players, the main risks are **counterparty concentration** (a handful of hyperscalers and AI labs), **stranded or idle assets** if AI demand or chip efficiency shifts, and **policy and tariff risk**: GCC subsidized power, Indian state incentives, US policy.

### Cited Findings
- **[Reported]** Oracle raised about **$43B of debt in FY2026**, and free cash flow was **-$23.7B**. Loans tied to an Oracle-leased New Mexico site (~$18B) were quoted at **89-91 cents on the dollar**, and Project Jupiter debt traded at about 90 cents — [iTiger, "Five debt hotspots"](https://www.itiger.com/news/2581711097); [Tom Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/is-your-ai-funded-by-junk-bonds/)
- **[Reported/Opinion]** Steve Eisman compares Oracle and Meta financing to the Enron era: off-balance-sheet structures protect credit ratings (he does not allege fraud). The Bank of England warns of risk pockets in opaque, illiquid assets. Polymarket put the probability of an AI downturn by 31 Dec at about 9% — [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/node/62022808); [iTiger](https://www.itiger.com/news/1128589106)
- **[Estimate]** Listed operators have de-rated to about **10.8x 2028E EBITDA**, 30-50% lower within months — [Blockspace](https://blockspace.media/insight/ai-data-center-lease-valuation-2026/)
- **[Reported]** Grid-dependent DC operators trade at **14-16x versus 20-24x** for those with contracted PPAs (trade press). - **[Estimate]** India's announced commitments (~$173B) far exceed what capacity forecasts require (~$110B for 6 GW by 2029). ### Inferences
- [Inference] Mitigants for IPPs and EPCs: (1) contract with investment-grade parents, not SPVs, or require parent guarantees. (2) Build **grid-connected, re-marketable** assets: a co-located solar+BESS park with an export connection can sell to the grid if the data center does not materialize, whereas a behind-the-meter-only asset is more exposed to stranding. (3) Use take-or-pay or capacity-reservation fees during the construction ramp. (4) Diversify across hyperscaler, colocation and sovereign-AI offtakers.
- [Inference] Technology risk: rack densities are rising and the industry is moving toward 800 V DC distribution and solid-state transformers. Legacy AC switchgear and UPS designs could age faster. EPCs should standardize modular designs that can be swapped later.
- [Inference] Tariff and trade risk: US tariffs on Chinese BESS cells and inverters raise US costs. India's ALMM and domestic-content rules for modules and cells affect solar EPC costs. Both are general industry context, not verified in this session.
- [Inference] Timing risk: Vertiv's revenue slippage and multi-year OEM backlogs mean cash conversion is slower than order headlines suggest, which matters for working capital in EPC contracts.
